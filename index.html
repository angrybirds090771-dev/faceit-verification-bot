FACEIT Verification Bot

A simple notification service for monitoring FACEIT account verification status.

About the project

FACEIT Verification Bot is a personal automation project designed to check the verification status of a FACEIT account and send a notification when the account becomes verified.

Verification monitoring service

How it works

The service periodically checks the verification status of the configured FACEIT account using the official FACEIT API.

When the account becomes verified, the service sends a notification through Telegram.
